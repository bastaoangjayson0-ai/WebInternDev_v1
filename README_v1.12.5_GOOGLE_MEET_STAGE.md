# WebInternDev v1.12.5 — Google Meet-style Stage

- Large shared screen on the left and participant grid on the right on desktop/laptop.
- Participant rail uses 3 columns on large desktop and 2 columns on smaller desktop/tablet widths.
- Small participant counts do not create a forced scrollbar.
- The scrollbar is enabled only for the large-call gallery when the participant tiles overflow the available gallery height.
- On phone/tablet, screen share becomes the top stage and participants are shown below.
- Meeting controls remain immediately below the stage.
- Active speaker priority and emote priority from v1.11.1 are preserved.
